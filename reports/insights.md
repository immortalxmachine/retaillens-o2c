# RetailLens - Order-to-Cash Insights (case study, real Olist data)

> Source: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (Kaggle olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce), CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 - real anonymised orders, mapped to SAP S/4HANA SD structures for a case study. Olist is a multi-category marketplace, not a fashion brand; the fashion & accessories segment is analysed separately for the case-study client. Generated by `src/insights.py` from the KPI tables of the latest pipeline run.

**Scope:** 98,353 orders, 112,279 order items, BRL 13.45M net revenue, orders placed 2017-01-01 to 2018-08-31. **Headline:** OTIF 92.9%, average lead time 12.4 days (promised 24.3), cancellation rate 0.5%, complaint rate 14.2%, payment approval 10.3 hours.

## 1. Customers in AL (Northeast) get the worst OTIF
**Evidence:** OTIF in AL is 79.1% vs 95.7% in PR (overall 92.9%). Orders there take 24.4 days, of which 20.8 are carrier transit. Across the Northeast, transit is 82.9% of the lead time, while seller hand-off (approval -> goods issue) takes only 2.8 days.

**Recommendation:** The bottleneck is the carrier leg, not the warehouse: renegotiate carrier SLAs for the Northeast, test a regional carrier or a stock point in the region, and keep the promised date (VDATU) realistic per state.

## 2. A late delivery is the strongest predictor of a complaint
**Evidence:** 62.5% of late orders get a 1-2 star review vs 9.3% of on-time orders (average score 2.27 vs 4.29). Late orders are only 6.7% of delivered, reviewed orders but cause 32.5% of their complaints.

**Recommendation:** Treat OTIF as a customer-experience KPI: trigger a proactive message and a small voucher when an order passes its promised date, and report OTIF and complaint rate side by side.

## 3. Office Furniture has the highest complaint rate
**Evidence:** 25.4% of office_furniture orders get a 1-2 star review (company 14.2%), although its OTIF is 91.9% - so it is not only a delivery problem. Freight is 27.4% of the item price. Products with no category in the master data rank #2 (20.1%) - a data-quality issue that also hurts customers.

**Recommendation:** Investigate office_furniture complaints by seller and product (assembly, damage in transit, description quality); there is no returns table in the source, so start capturing return reasons (order reason AUGRU on return orders).

## 4. Fashion & accessories: small share, but better economics
**Evidence:** The fashion segment is 11.4% of net revenue with an average item price of BRL 158 (rest BRL 117). Freight is 22.6% of the price vs 33.0%, complaints 13.7% vs 15.3%, OTIF 93.3% vs 92.9%.

**Recommendation:** For a fashion-retail roll-out, the segment is a good pilot: light parcels keep freight low. Focus the pilot on watches and bags (the top fashion sellers) and add size/fit return reasons before scaling clothing.

## 5. Boleto payments slow down the start of fulfilment
**Evidence:** Credit cards carry 78.4% of the paid value and are approved after 4.5 hours on average (66.8% paid in instalments, 3.5 on average). Boleto (17.9% of value) takes 33.1 hours and 57.0% need more than a day - the order cannot ship before the payment is cleared.

**Recommendation:** Nudge boleto customers to instant payment methods (e.g. PIX, launched in Brazil after this data period) and promise delivery dates from payment approval, not from purchase, for boleto orders.
